what if I try to fetch reviews from amazon or google maps? are they also protected from bots?

Yes, both are protected, and arguably harder than Trustpilot. This is from general knowledge of those platforms, and I can’t test any of it from my sandbox, so treat it as likely rather than verified.

Amazon

Aggressive bot detection: CAPTCHAs, IP reputation checks and fingerprinting. A plain fetch from a server gets blocked quickly.
Since 2024, most reviews sit behind a login. The product page shows only a handful, and the full “see all reviews” pages usually require signing in. Scraping while logged in also puts your account at risk.
The terms of service prohibit scraping. There is no public reviews API for outsiders.

Google Maps

The page is heavily JavaScript-rendered. Reviews load through internal calls as you scroll, so you’d need a headless browser, and Google also flags automated traffic.
The official Places API is the legitimate route, but it returns only about 5 reviews per place, which is too thin for trend analysis.
The terms of service restrict scraping here too.

G2 and Capterra also use strong bot protection (Cloudflare-style challenges), so they’re about as hard as Amazon.
