That 403 is Trustpilot blocking the request, not a bug in your setup. It sits behind bot protection, and Node’s built-in fetch is easily recognised as non-browser traffic, so changing the User-Agent is unlikely to fix it on its own. I couldn’t get through from my sandbox either, so I can’t promise which approach gets past it from your server.
